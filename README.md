# Reverie Weaver: website

The marketing, support and privacy pages for Reverie Weaver, a habit and focus app for your scattered days, on iPhone and iPad, published by Reverie Archive.

- `index.html`: the landing page
- `guide.html`: the Gentle Guide
- `press.html`: the press kit
- `support.html`: support and purchase help
- `privacy.html`: the privacy policy

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step. The pages use no cookies, analytics or third-party resources.
