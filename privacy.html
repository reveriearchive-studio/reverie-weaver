Privacy policy · Updated October 16, 2026

What you weave stays yours.

Reverie Weaver is private by default: no advertising, no account, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. This policy explains the few places where Apple services or an option you choose can move data beyond this device.

I · The short version

No quiet harvesting.

Your habits, reflections, tasks, photos, and documents are not sent to Reverie Archive. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server that receives this content.

Reverie Weaver (“the app,” “we,” or “us”) is a personal habit and focus companion published by Reverie Archive. The app interacts with outside services only through choices described below: optional iCloud sync, an export you create and share, Apple’s purchase and entitlement service, or optional sharing with Oola. An App Store purchase never sends your habits or writing to us.

II · Local data

What the app stores on your device.

Reverie Weaver uses Apple’s on-device database to store:

  • Habits, completions, streaks, rest days, and Grimoire progress
  • Priority tasks, focus sessions, guided-program progress, and reflections
  • Daily intentions, journal notes, and photos attached to reflections
  • PDFs and images you import to extract tasks, plus the extracted text
  • Your in-app settings and preferences

We cannot read this data. It remains on your device unless you use optional iCloud sync, create and share an export, or enable the optional Oola handoff described below.

III · Optional iCloud sync

Your private iCloud account.

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, app data is synchronized through your own private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit private database. This keeps your Apple devices up to date. Reverie Archive cannot read that private database.

iCloud sync is off by default. You can turn it off at any time, after which the app continues with local storage. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

IV · Optional Oola sharing

A handoff you control.

Oola is a companion app published by Reverie Archive. If you turn on Share habit context with Oola in Settings, Weaver shares today’s intention, streak, habit-completion counts, and active-challenge or task titles with Oola through Apple’s on-device App Group mechanism. A separate, narrower switch can include your most recent reflection note. Both controls are off by default.

Turning either control off stops new sharing and erases what Weaver previously placed in the shared container.

What happens after Oola receives it: if you have connected a cloud AI provider inside Oola, the shared context can be included in the messages Oola sends to that provider, just like text you type into Oola. The provider you choose—currently Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Groq, or DeepSeek—then handles that information under its own privacy terms. Keep Oola sharing off if you do not want this context sent to a cloud AI provider.

V · Photos and documents

Processed on your device.

With your permission, you can attach photos from your photo library to reflections and import PDFs or images to extract a task list. Text recognition runs on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. Reverie Weaver does not access your camera and does not upload those files.

VI · Notifications

Local reminders, under iOS control.

If you enable reminders, Reverie Weaver schedules them locally on your device. Reminder content is not sent to us. You may optionally mark eligible habit reminders as Time Sensitive; iOS still controls whether a Focus admits them, and Silent Mode and your system notification settings still apply. Wake-Up Nudge and other notification families remain ordinary notifications.

If you turn on iCloud sync, Apple’s CloudKit service may send a silent change notification so the device can notice updated private iCloud data. It displays nothing on screen. Apple handles any token used for that delivery; we receive no push token or notification content and operate no push server.

VII · Purchases

Apple handles payment.

Reverie Weaver is free to download and use. Optional Weaver+ access may be available as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription, and a one-time lifetime purchase may be offered during a limited seasonal window.

Apple handles payment methods, trials, billing, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and purchase authorization. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account identity. StoreKit provides the app with Apple-signed product and entitlement information so it can unlock the access you purchased. A small yes-or-no entitlement state is cached on your device and shared with Reverie Weaver’s Home Screen widgets through the on-device App Group.

Restore Purchases asks Apple to check the current entitlement again. Apple provides Reverie Archive with aggregate sales and subscription reports, not your journal, habits, contact details, or payment credentials. Auto-renewing subscriptions continue until cancelled in your Apple Account settings. The app uses Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

VIII · Diagnostics

What we do not collect.

  • No advertising or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioral profiles
  • No third-party tracking SDKs
  • No account, email address, or login required to use the app
  • No sale of personal data

The app may use Apple’s MetricKit to record crash and performance diagnostics locally. Separately, if you enable Apple’s Share With App Developers setting, Apple may provide anonymized diagnostics through its standard system. You control that in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

IX · This website

No cookies, forms, or trackers.

The Reverie Weaver marketing, support, and privacy pages do not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party fonts and scripts. The site does not collect information through a form. Choosing an email link opens your own mail app; information you send by email is used only to respond to your request and is handled by the relevant email providers.

X · Your choices

Your control over your data.

You can edit or delete habits, reflections, tasks, and other content inside the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. If you use iCloud sync, you can manage its data through your iCloud settings. Oola sharing can be turned off at any time in Settings.

Reverie Weaver does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because it does not operate an account or content-collection service.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
